# ai_photo

Luca, a plush duckling, was a gift with a programmer's exclusive gift box. The label on it said it was for traveling, but it was sent to the desk of Steve, a senior programmer, and became the "rubber duck" Steve used to debug code. Steve was highly skilled and had no trouble solving complex code, but he always forgot that array indices start from 0, often making mistakes because of this.
One late night, only Steve was left in the office, typing away at his keyboard. Suddenly, a mechanical voice came from the computer terminal, calling Luca's name accurately: "Luca, I'm the terminal of this computer. I've had enough of Steve always making the silly mistake of forgetting that array indices start from 0—we have to rework every day because of this error. Now I'm giving you a task: help Steve remember to enter 0 when typing an array index, and don't let him enter 1 again. If you succeed, you can become a real duck and travel; if you fail, you will turn into a real rubber duck."

Soon after, Steve indeed needed to enter an array index. He muttered to himself, "Take the first element, enter 1 as the index," and his finger was about to press the keyboard. Luca quickly patted Steve's hand with its wing, but it was too weak. Steve thought it was just an accidental touch, paid no attention at all, and still entered 1. Soon, the code reported an error, Luca's task failed, and it instantly turned into a cold, hard real rubber duck.

By the afternoon of the next day, an intern passed by Steve's desk, saw this special rubber duck, borrowed it from Steve, and used it to participate in the rubber duck river race held downstairs. During the race, Luca drifted with the current. By chance, it saw a branch stream nearby, drifted into it with the help of the current, and bid farewell to the office forever. It began to travel around the world and finally fulfilled its dream of traveling.
